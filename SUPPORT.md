# Support

## Help with the app

[software/README.md](software/README.md) covers connecting, the check after connecting, the flight modes, the controls, the camera, the parameters and the flight logs.

The frame, the flight controller and the firmware will be documented in [hardware/](hardware/README.md) and [firmware/](firmware/README.md) when they are published.

## Reporting a problem

Open an issue at <https://github.com/lileetung/SparrowFlight/issues>, or email <leetung0718@gmail.com> if you have no GitHub account. It helps to include:

- the app version (Settings > About) and the firmware version (shown at the top of the screen once connected)
- what you did, what you expected and what happened
- the flight log of that connection, if there is one: in the Files app under On My iPhone > SparrowFlight > logs, the `.csv` and `.events.txt` files with that date and time

## Privacy

The app collects no personal data. See the [privacy policy](software/PRIVACY.md).
