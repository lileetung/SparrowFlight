# Contributing to SparrowFlight

Thank you for your interest in SparrowFlight.

## Current stage

The frame, the flight controller and the firmware have not been published yet. Until they are, this repository holds the project documentation and the guide to the companion app, and pull requests for hardware or firmware cannot be accepted.

## What helps now

- **Problem reports** about the app or the documentation: open an [issue](https://github.com/lileetung/SparrowFlight/issues/new/choose). The bug report form asks for the app and firmware versions and the flight log, which [SUPPORT.md](SUPPORT.md) explains how to find.
- **Questions and suggestions:** open an issue as well.
- **Corrections to the documentation:** typos, unclear steps or wrong values, as an issue or a pull request.

## After the release

Guidelines for hardware and firmware contributions (building, testing, and the files a change has to update) will be added here when those parts are published. A contribution is accepted under the licence of the part it changes; see [LICENSE.md](LICENSE.md).

## Code of Conduct

Everyone taking part in SparrowFlight is expected to follow the [Code of Conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).
