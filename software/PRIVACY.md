# SparrowFlight privacy policy

Last updated: 2 November 2026

SparrowFlight ("the app") is the iPhone app that flies Sparrow drones. It is made by lileetung.

## In short

The app does not collect personal data. It has no accounts, no servers, no advertising and no analytics. It sends nothing to the developer, or to anyone else, over the internet.

## What the app uses on the phone

- **Bluetooth**, to list nearby Sparrow drones. The list comes from what each drone broadcasts (its ID, firmware version and battery) and the signal strength the phone measures. The app does not connect to drones or send them anything over Bluetooth.
- **WiFi**, to join the WiFi network of the drone you pick. iOS asks you first.
- **Local network**, to fly the drone over its WiFi: the app sends stick movements and the commands you choose, and receives the drone's flight data and camera picture.

The camera picture comes from the drone's own camera. It is shown on the screen and is not saved or sent anywhere. The app does not use the phone's camera, microphone, location, contacts or photos.

## What stays on the phone

- **Flight logs.** For each connection the app writes two files to its `logs` folder: the drone's flight data, and a list of events and the commands sent. They include the drone's ID and firmware version, and the phone's address on the drone's WiFi network. You can view, share or delete them in the app, or in the Files app under On My iPhone > SparrowFlight > logs. They leave the phone only when you share them yourself.
- **Settings.** The language, flight mode, stick settings, whether the camera picture is shown, and an optional IP address.

Deleting the app deletes all of these.

The drone keeps its own settings and a record of its last flights in its memory. These stay on the drone.

## Sharing

No data is sold or shared with anyone. The app does not track you across apps or websites.
